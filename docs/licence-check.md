# Licence check (2 November 2026)

Every pack that `Assets/Art/CREDITS.md` marked "to confirm" (45 of 60 entries), checked against the asset
library (licence files, details stored in model files), Sketchfab's public listing, the itch.io pages, and what
the director sent (chilly_durango's page, valsekamerplant's pages). `CREDITS.md` has the details per pack.
**Nothing here is legal advice**: before a release, read each pack's own page yourself.

What the licences mean, in short:

- **CC0:** public domain. Use, change, sell, no credit needed.
- **CC Attribution (CC BY 4.0):** free to use in any game, paid or not, and to change. You must credit the
  author (name, a link, the licence name, and that you changed it if you did).
- **CC Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA 4.0):** the same, plus: if you share the *changed model itself*, it
  must be under the same licence. Shipping it inside a game is normally fine.
- **Sketchfab Free Standard:** use in your projects, including commercial ones; no credit; no redistribution.
- **Pixabay Content Licence:** free, including commercial use; no credit; don't hand out the sound on its own.
